## Items awaiting confirmation

Existing wording and controls remain in place for these items; this list does not certify them for publication.

- **Partner scope and status:** Approve active versus prospective status, descriptions and logo use for Sunnybrook’s Veterans Program, the Schulich Heart Program, CAMH and MoCA. The draft proposes separate Sunnybrook program cards and more specific homepage naming; those changes are deferred. Confirm CAMH's STOP, memory, lung-cancer access and Indigenous service pathways before changing the current descriptions.
- **Consent and follow-up:** Confirm whether the app is mandatory, options for people without smartphones, which consenting clients receive calls, and the exact sharing permissions. The existing step-three body remains unchanged; the draft proposes “You decide if you want to share your screening results…” instead.
- **Privacy and AI:** Confirm the controller, hosting, retention, withdrawal, AI processing and opt-out, audit access and breach response. Obtain privacy and clinical review of the current privacy page and technology copy.
- **Public claims:** Confirm the Legion history/accessibility statements and claims such as free, secure, clinical-grade, clinically validated, available now and no health card required. The existing 2.5-million primary-care statistic still needs a source and an “as of” date, or a decision to remove it.
- **Locations:** Supply actual public access hours and accessibility details before adding those fields. Confirm the new venue addresses, both Downtown Toronto sites, and whether “Beavertown” means Beaverton. New pins remain approximate and omit directions until addresses are confirmed.
- **Contact and app links:** Supply contact details, a working form destination, a partner inquiry destination and real App Store/Google Play URLs. Existing placeholders remain.
- **French and launch review:** French equivalents now cover the updated step label, directions, map tooltips/popups, location cards, Toronto names, pending venue notes, homepage map teaser and privacy page. These are draft translations of the retained English wording and still need language review. Obtain final clinical, privacy, partner-brand and accessibility approval.
