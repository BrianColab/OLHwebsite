2. Description of the Ontario Legion Health App and Websites

Ontario Legion Health is a guided health screening program offered to members of the Royal Canadian Legion at participating Legion Posts across Ontario. The program combines a veteran-facing mobile application, a web-based nurse portal, and an on-site PharmaSmart kiosk that captures biometric measurements at participating Posts.

Through the App, eligible members complete a structured health screening questionnaire and, where a kiosk is present, contribute biometric readings captured at the Post. Screening responses are reviewed by registered nurses, who may follow up with participants where appropriate. TryCycle acts as the backend operator and system of record for the program.

Ontario Legion Health provides health screening and nurse review. It is not a provider of emergency services and is not a substitute for the care of your own physician or health care team.
